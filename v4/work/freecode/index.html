What it is

Free classes in Scratch, then Python, for children aged eight and up who do not have access to those resources or cannot get to them. Sessions are monthly and in person at Nova Southeastern University’s Alvin Sherman Library.

How far it has come

Across the first four sessions the program reached more than 40 students, and I taught around ten of them myself. Classes grew from three or four participants to six to ten.

My role

I help teach, organize the sessions and support the student team. From Groton I teach remotely and coordinate the program. A photograph of a session at the library will be added when it is supplied.
