V

Research

Two student projects exploring drug safety and rehabilitation, with an emphasis on methods, evidence and the limits of a model.

2026 · Independent research · Manuscript submitted

Analyzed 486 FDA adverse-event cases for aducanumab from 2016 to 2024 and compared reporting patterns with lecanemab and donanemab. Advised by Dr. Sujata K. Bhatia of Harvard University.

Conducted the analysis and wrote the manuscript with Dr. Bhatia’s guidance on background, methods and presentation. Submitted to the Scholarly Review Journal; publication is pending.

The study examined amyloid-related imaging abnormalities (ARIA), including brain swelling and bleeding visible on imaging. ARIA appeared in 37.2% of the analyzed aducanumab cases, compared with 25.7% for lecanemab and 34.0% for donanemab in the comparison datasets.

These percentages describe submitted reports, not the likelihood of an event in treated patients. Reporting patterns alone cannot establish causation or compare the drugs’ clinical safety. FDA guidance on interpreting adverse-event reports.

The project grew from a family experience with Alzheimer’s disease.

Manuscript: Amyloid-Related Imaging Abnormalities Dominate Aducanumab’s Complete Post-Marketing Record: A Comparative Pharmacovigilance Analysis.

Summer 2026 · Knee-exoskeleton design and simulation

Designed and simulated a knee exoskeleton that adjusts its assistance during movement. An individual project with guidance from Georgia Tech’s Dr. Aaron Young and doctoral student Dongho Park of the EPIC Lab.

Explored assistance during squat-to-stand movement after ACL reconstruction. The controller uses knee angle, movement speed and a leg-asymmetry term to adjust support. A logistic-regression classifier identifies the movement phase.

Values above 100% indicate overshoot of the model’s target. Adaptive assistance tapered by 11.8% as the modeled capacity increased.

These are results from a simplified simulation with a prescribed recovery curve, not patient outcomes. Hardware testing and clinical validation remain future work. Lower modeled effort alone does not establish better rehabilitation.

Produced an IEEE-format report and a hand-drawn hardware layout. Presented August 23, 2026. Playing three sports helped motivate the interest in rehabilitation.
