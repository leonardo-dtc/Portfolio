Daedalus is set inside the Labyrinth as Daedalus’s still-running machine. Players are meant to need each other: the game is built so that isolation is what it punishes. Parts of the labyrinth need more than one pair of hands.

We wrote a full design document before building: the systems, a bestiary, the items and a story with two endings. The rule for all of it is to show rather than tell. Every mechanic has a cause in the fiction, and the dread is meant to come from scale and wrongness rather than speed.

- Team: Three people, under the studio name Gompurkle
- My part: Main builder. Design and implementation are shared as the project develops.
- Built on: Roblox
- Status: At the design stage, working toward an alpha. No release date has been announced.
