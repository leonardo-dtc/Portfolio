About me

Goaltender · Student researcher · Violist

Read early, then commit.

I was born in Beijing and grew up in Ontario and South Florida. Since 2025 I have boarded at Groton School in Massachusetts, where my week moves between the rink, the classroom and rehearsal. In goal I am not the biggest goaltender on the ice, so I rely on reading the play, positioning and skating. In research, the question I keep coming back to is how the body fails and how it recovers. Most of what I make outside class is shared: concerts with OCAPEX, coding classes for kids, projects with friends. I plan to study biomedical engineering.

DrugSafety

Research · 2026 · Pharmacovigilance · Manuscript submitted

I analyzed the 486 FDA adverse-event cases filed for aducanumab from 2016 to 2024 and compared their reporting patterns with lecanemab and donanemab, using chi-squared tests, two-proportion z-tests and exact binomial tests in Python with SciPy. Independent work, sole author, advised by Dr. Sujata K. Bhatia of Harvard University. Not an efficacy study.

Manuscript submitted to the Scholarly Review Journal; publication pending.

KneeExoskeleton

Genuvalens · Summer 2026 · Design and simulation · Guidance from the EPIC Lab, Georgia Tech

I designed and simulated an assist-as-needed knee exoskeleton for squat-to-stand movement after ACL reconstruction: an impedance controller with a leg-asymmetry term and a logistic-regression phase classifier. Model results, not patient outcomes. Seven-page IEEE-format report, presented August 23, 2026.

  • NowGame design

    a labyrinth that changes while you are in it

    Daedalus, a cooperative survival-horror game for two to six players, in development.

    Daedalus is set inside the Labyrinth as Daedalus’s still-running machine. Players are meant to need each other: the game is built so that isolation is what it punishes. Parts of the labyrinth need more than one pair of hands, and the labyrinth itself changes.

    We wrote a full design document before building: the systems, a bestiary, the items and a story with two endings. The rule for all of it is to show rather than tell. Every mechanic has a cause in the fiction, and the dread is meant to come from scale and wrongness rather than speed.

    Team
    Three people, under the studio name Gompurkle
    My part
    Main builder. Design and implementation are shared across the three of us as the project develops.
