How I play

I am a butterfly goaltender who relies on positioning, reflexes and skating rather than size, and on reading the play early. In 2025-26 I was the second goaltender at Groton; this season I am the starter. College hockey is the goal, and Division I is the long-term goal.

I train with goaltending coach Patrick Spano in Florida in the summer and take his guidance remotely during the school year, and I help coach the younger goalies at his clinics. This year’s work is reflexes, agility and balance.

Registered with the NCAA Eligibility Center and USA Hockey. Registered with Hockey Canada before moving to Florida in 2019.
