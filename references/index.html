    Filling this in

    Where the copy lives

    One object, RESUME, at the top of the script, inside a comment banner. Identity and sections are all there, and the markup is generated from it.

    Each section owns its own figures. The first one is struck onto that section's plate on the front page; the rest appear in the record band at the head of the page it belongs to.

    Add a fourth section and the plates, the roman numerals, the tabs and the next/previous links all follow on their own. No other edit.

    How to write a bullet

    Verb, then scope, then the number. Anchored the 4×800 relay to a school record of 7:58.21 at the district final. Never more than two lines.

    Wrap the figure in <b> to lift it from grey to white. That is the entire emphasis system, and it is what lets a coach skim a page for numbers alone.

    One phrase in the front-page role line can be wrapped in <em> to take the gold.

    What not to touch

    Everything below the RESUME object. The palette, the four roles gold is allowed to play, the type scale and the motion are the design — changing one unbalances the others.

    Four figures in total, counted across every section, and only figures a coach or an admissions reader would care about cold. The front page does not scroll; anything added to it has to earn its height.
