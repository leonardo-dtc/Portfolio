2026

Pharmacovigilance · first author · Scholarly Review Journal

A comparative pharmacovigilance analysis of every FDA adverse-event report filed for aducanumab, the Alzheimer’s antibody approved on amyloid clearance rather than cognitive benefit: 486 cases, 2016 to 2024, set beside its successors lecanemab and donanemab.

Amyloid-Related Imaging Abnormalities Dominate Aducanumab’s Complete Post-Marketing Record: A Comparative Pharmacovigilance Analysis. Researched, analysed and written solo, with the guidance of Dr. Sujata K. Bhatia, MD, PhD, PE, of Harvard University.

The results show amyloid-related imaging abnormalities (ARIA) in 37.2% of cases, against 25.7% for lecanemab and 34.0% for donanemab; ARIA reported by healthcare professionals far more often than other events (69.6% compared to 48.2%); and a near-even sex split among reports for a disease that mostly affects women, the mark of a reporting stream that came from the trials rather than the clinic. Four hypotheses, four tests.

Pharmacovigilance is the part of a drug’s life that begins after the trials end. Aducanumab’s life was brief, approved in 2021 over the advice of its own advisory committee, withdrawn in 2024.

The question started at home, when Alzheimer’s disease took a grandfather.

Summer 2026

Georgia Tech · Dr. Aaron Young · Dongho Park, EPIC Lab

An assist-as-needed knee exoskeleton for the months after ACL reconstruction, designed and simulated as a solo project at Georgia Tech under Dr. Aaron Young and doctoral student Dongho Park of the EPIC Lab.

Reconstruction repairs the ligament; it does not repair the quadriceps inhibition around it. The design question, how much to help and when to take the help away, is answered by a torque law with an added asymmetry term, switched by a logistic-regression phase classifier reading knee angle and velocity each timestep. The instructors called the asymmetry term an original idea and the concept that makes the controller adaptive in a meaningful sense.

In a two-leg squat-to-stand simulation, no support achieved 87.2% extension at 9.6 N·m from the athlete; fixed assist overshot to 113.9% at 23.3 N·m; adaptive assist extended to 98.0% at 3.6 N·m, tapering 11.8% as capacity recovered. Seven-page IEEE-format report created, with five figures, two tables, and a hardware layout drawn by hand; presented August 23, 2026.

The personal affiliation is three sports, all of them requiring heavy legwork. ACL tears are common in each one, and rehabilitation is the part nobody designs for. Named for the genu, the knee, and valens, healthy.

Why

Pharmaceuticals · rehabilitation robotics · biomedical engineering

Both projects are the same question from two sides: what a treatment does to a body once it leaves the controlled setting, and how a machine should help a body heal without doing the work for it.

The interest is the cell: its structure, its failure, and the point where treating that failure becomes an engineering problem rather than only a biological one. Biomedical engineering is where the two roads meet.
