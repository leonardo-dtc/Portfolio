3b

OC Apex

OC APEX (ocapex.com) was founded in 2022 in South Florida as a nonprofit music organization led by students, devoted to spreading the joy of music. Musicians range from ages 6 to 20, and bring lively performances and music education to elderly care homes, rehabilitation centers, and underprivileged communities. What began in 2022 as a small group of student performers visiting a single senior living community has grown into a program that runs throughout the year, with dozens of active volunteers and more than two dozen community partners across South Florida. Every performance is planned, rehearsed, and led entirely by its student members.

Youtube Footage of Events (www.youtube.com/@OCApex)

In 2026 alone OCApex has raised more than $8,500 for various children’s foundations.
