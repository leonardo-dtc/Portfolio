Status. Daedalus is at the design stage. There is no released gameplay to show yet, so the image slots on this page are placeholders.

01

Premise

A first-person cooperative survival-horror game for two to six players, set inside the Labyrinth as Daedalus’s still-running machine. Players are meant to need each other: the game is built so that isolation is what it punishes.

02

Approach

We wrote a full design document before building: the systems, a bestiary, the items and a story with two endings. The rule for all of it is to show rather than tell. Every mechanic has a cause in the fiction, and the dread is meant to come from scale and wrongness rather than speed.

Interaction system

Cooperation is the core interaction: parts of the labyrinth need more than one pair of hands, and the labyrinth itself changes. A short loop of a two-player mechanism will be added here once it exists.
