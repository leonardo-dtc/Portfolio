transition options

Four ways off the hero.

The glyph wave and scramble-settle are now the hero's alone. These are the candidates for everything else — the page-to-page transition and the subsection expansion. Hit page to replay a panel arriving, and subsection (or any title) to open a row. Each is built from the tokens the real site already uses.

my read

3 for pages, 2 for subsections. The buffer print is the most honestly terminal thing here and it is the only one that stays legible while it runs — you can start reading before it finishes, which matters on a panel someone is scanning for facts. It is also clearly a different gesture from the hero's glyph wave rather than a quieter version of it.

The dolly earns the subsection, because that is the one place a camera metaphor is literally true: you are pushing into one item and letting its neighbours fall out of focus. Used on whole pages it starts to feel like a slideshow.

  • 1 · CRT sweep — the most visually impressive in isolation, and the most tiring on the fifth visit. The bloom also fights the field behind it.
  • 2 · Camera dolly — the blur is expensive to composite full-page; scoped to one row it is cheap and reads beautifully.
  • 3 · Buffer print — cheapest, calmest, most readable, most terminal. Scales to any amount of content.
  • 4 · Column shutter — mechanical and striking, but it hides the content until it is done, and it reads more departure-board than terminal.

They can also be mixed — that is what I would do.
