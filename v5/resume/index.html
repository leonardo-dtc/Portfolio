Research and experience

  • Aducanumab pharmacovigilance analysis

    2026

    Independent research, sole author. Advised by Dr. Sujata K. Bhatia, Harvard University

    I analyzed the 486 FDA adverse-event cases filed for aducanumab from 2016 to 2024 and compared the reporting patterns with lecanemab and donanemab: chi-squared tests, two-proportion z-tests and exact binomial tests in Python with SciPy. ARIA appeared in 37.2% of the aducanumab cases, against 25.7% and 34.0% in the comparison sets; these are proportions of submitted reports, not patient risk. Manuscript Amyloid-Related Imaging Abnormalities Dominate Aducanumab’s Complete Post-Marketing Record: A Comparative Pharmacovigilance Analysis submitted to the Scholarly Review Journal; publication pending. Project page.

  • Genuvalens, knee-exoskeleton design and simulation

    Summer 2026

    Individual project. Guidance from Dr. Aaron Young and doctoral student Dongho Park, EPIC Lab, Georgia Tech

    I designed and simulated an assist-as-needed knee exoskeleton for squat-to-stand movement after ACL reconstruction: an impedance controller with a leg-asymmetry term and a logistic-regression phase classifier. In a simplified five-repetition simulation, adaptive assistance reached 98.0% of target extension at 3.6 N·m peak athlete torque, against 87.2% at 9.6 N·m unassisted and 113.9% at 23.3 N·m with fixed assistance. Model results, not patient outcomes. Seven-page IEEE-format report and hand-drawn hardware layout; presented August 23, 2026. Project page.
